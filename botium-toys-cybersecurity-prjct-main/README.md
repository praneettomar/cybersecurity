# Botium Toys: Cybersecurity Internal Audit and Risk Assessment

## 1. Project Overview

This project presents a cybersecurity internal audit and risk assessment of Botium Toys, a fictional United States-based toy retailer with a physical store, office, warehouse, and growing international e-commerce business.

The assessment examines the company's documented security controls, identifies gaps in its current security posture, reviews relevant compliance practices, and proposes a practical remediation roadmap.

The objective is to understand how cybersecurity weaknesses can affect business operations, customer information, regulatory obligations, and business continuity.

**Project type:** Cybersecurity audit and risk assessment  
**Business sector:** Retail and e-commerce  
**Framework:** NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)  
**Security principles:** Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability (CIA triad)  
**Compliance areas:** GDPR, PCI DSS, and selected SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria  
**Environment:** Fictional business scenario

## 2. Business Context

Botium Toys operates from a single physical location that serves as its main office, retail storefront, and warehouse. Its online business has expanded to serve customers in the United States and other countries.

The original case identifies weaknesses in access management, encryption, data backups, disaster recovery, intrusion detection, and asset management.

The source report assigns an overall risk score of 8 out of 10.

These weaknesses may affect customer data protection, payment security, business continuity, and the company's ability to meet applicable legal and contractual obligations.

## 3. Project Objectives

This assessment aims to:

- Understand the business environment and identify relevant assets.
- Review existing administrative, technical, and physical security controls.
- Evaluate risks to the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information.
- Identify weaknesses in access management, data protection, monitoring, and recovery.
- Assess relevant privacy, payment security, and assurance requirements.
- Develop a structured risk register.
- Prioritize remediation based on business impact and risk.
- Recommend measurable actions to improve the security posture.

## 4. Assessment Approach

The project follows a structured assessment process:

1. Define the audit scope, objectives, and limitations.
2. Identify business assets and information that require protection.
3. Assess documented security controls.
4. Evaluate risks using a consistent scoring methodology.
5. Map relevant findings to recognised cybersecurity frameworks.
6. Review the applicability of relevant compliance requirements.
7. Develop a prioritized remediation roadmap.
8. Define measures for tracking progress and validating control effectiveness.

The assessment uses the information provided in the original case study. Additional risk ratings, framework mappings, and recommendations are identified as independent analytical work.

## 5. Frameworks and Principles

### NIST Cybersecurity Framework

The NIST Cybersecurity Framework provides a structure for understanding and improving cybersecurity risk management.

The assessment uses the six functions of NIST CSF 2.0:

- Govern
- Identify
- Protect
- Detect
- Respond
- Recover

### CIA Triad

The CIA triad is used to assess the potential impact of security weaknesses on:

- **Confidentiality:** Preventing unauthorized access to information.
- **Integrity:** Protecting information from unauthorized or improper changes.
- **Availability:** Ensuring systems and information are available when needed.

### Additional References

The project also considers:

- GDPR, where its territorial and material scope applies.
- PCI DSS, where the company stores, processes, or transmits payment card data.
- SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria as a reference for evaluating security, availability, confidentiality, processing integrity, and privacy.
- Risk-based control prioritization and defence-in-depth principles.

Framework references do not, by themselves, establish compliance or certification.

## 6. Key Areas of Investigation

The assessment focuses on:

- Identity and access management

- Network security and threat detection
- Backup and disaster recovery
- Asset inventory and classification
- Legacy system maintenance
- Physical security
- Privacy and payment data protection
- Incident response and regulatory notification
- Governance and ongoing control monitoring

## 9. Limitations and Professional Disclaimer

Botium Toys is a fictional company used in a learning case study.

This project is a documented assessment based on the supplied scenario and additional analysis. No live systems were accessed or tested. No penetration testing, vulnerability scanning, forensic investigation, or independent compliance audit was performed.

The assessment does not establish that Botium Toys, or any real organization, is compliant or non-compliant with a particular regulation or standard.

All additional ratings, proposed control mappings, and remediation priorities must be understood in the context of the available information.

## 10. Learning Objectives

This project demonstrates the application of cybersecurity risk assessment principles, security control analysis, compliance awareness, and business-focused remediation planning.

It is intended to demonstrate the ability to translate technical security weaknesses into understandable business risks and actionable recommendations.
