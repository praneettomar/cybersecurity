# 04. Risk Assessment Methodology

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the methodology used to identify, evaluate, prioritise, and treat cybersecurity risks in the Botium Toys assessment.

The methodology provides a consistent approach to evaluating the potential impact of security weaknesses on business operations, sensitive information, and technology assets.

The approach is informed by NIST risk assessment guidance and the NIST Cybersecurity Framework. The numerical scoring model described below is a proposed method for this portfolio exercise, not an official NIST scoring model.

## 2. Risk Assessment Approach

The assessment follows these steps:

1. Identify relevant business and technology assets.
2. Identify threats and security weaknesses that could affect those assets.
3. Describe potential risk events and their business consequences.
4. Estimate the likelihood and impact of each risk.
5. Calculate a risk score and assign a risk rating.
6. Recommend suitable risk treatment actions.
7. Identify an accountable risk owner.
8. Review the risk after proposed controls are implemented and validated.

The risk register in `05-risk-register.csv` records the results of this process.

## 3. Risk Scoring Model

This assessment uses a proposed five-point likelihood scale and five-point impact scale.

### 3.1 Likelihood Scale

| Score | Rating | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rare | The event is considered highly unlikely under current conditions. |
| 2 | Unlikely | The event could occur, but is not expected frequently. |
| 3 | Possible | The event could occur under plausible circumstances. |
| 4 | Likely | The event is reasonably expected to occur given the identified conditions. |
| 5 | Almost Certain | The event is expected to occur frequently or is highly probable. |

These ratings are qualitative estimates. They are not derived from measured incident frequencies.
